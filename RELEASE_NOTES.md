Xstream TV for Windows, early preview. Two downloads, both native (no emulation):

- **Xstream-TV-windows-x64.zip**: Intel and AMD PCs
- **Xstream-TV-windows-arm64.zip**: Snapdragon / ARM PCs

What's in it:
- The Xstream start animation and logo, the same as on phones
- Home with hero cards and poster rows
- Title details with seasons and episodes
- Search with on-screen and physical keyboard
- Sources list, best quality first ("Instant Download" and "10Gbps" links filtered out)
- Video player with seek, pause, audio and subtitle choice; hold ◀ / ▶ to scrub with a picture preview

Add your stream addon links to `%APPDATA%\xe\xe\addons.txt` (one per line), then restart.
